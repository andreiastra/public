# BBQ Chicken Pizza

## Layer Order

1. Pizza dough/base
2. Thin layer of BBQ sauce
3. --
4. Cooked chicken (mixed with a little BBQ sauce)
5. Red onion and any other toppings
6. Small Mozzarella ball

## Baking

Bake at **220°C for 10–15 minutes**, until the cheese is melted and the crust is golden.

## Comments

- do not put too much toppings
- pat chiken dry
- small Mozzarella ball is fine
- try 12 mins with fan
