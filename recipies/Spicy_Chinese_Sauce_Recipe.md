# Spicy Chinese-Style Sauce

## Ingredients

-   4 tbsp soy sauce
-   1 tbsp honey
-   1 tsp rice vinegar
-   1 clove garlic, finely minced
-   1 tsp fresh grated ginger
-   1/4 tsp chili powder (add more to taste)
-   1 tsp sesame oil (optional)

## Instructions

1.  Mix the soy sauce, honey, and rice vinegar until the honey
    dissolves.
2.  Stir in the garlic, ginger, and chili powder.
3.  Add the sesame oil, if using.
4.  Taste and adjust:
    -   More chili powder for extra heat.
    -   More honey if too spicy.
    -   A little more soy sauce for a stronger savory flavor.
5.  Let the sauce sit for 5--10 minutes before serving.

## Great With

-   Stir-fried vegetables
-   Chicken
-   Beef
-   Noodles
-   Rice
-   Dumplings
