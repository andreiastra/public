# Love Your Coast Photography Competition: Strategic Guide

An overview of the rules, timelines, categories, and technical requirements for the Clean Coasts 'Love Your Coast' Photography Competition, tailored for coastal and underwater photography in Ireland.

## 📅 Timeline & Submission Window

The competition operates on an annual cycle, celebrating stunning imagery of Ireland's coastline and waterways.

* **Launch Date:** Submissions typically open on Valentine’s Day (**February 14th**).
* **Closing Date:** Mid-May (The 2026 deadline was **May 15th, 2026**).
* **Eligibility Window (History):** Photos must have been captured within the **last 24 months** prior to the entry deadline. This allows for extensive archiving and planning across multiple diving or field seasons.
* **Current Status (2026):** **Closed & Awarded**. The 2026 ceremony took place on July 16, 2026, at Dublin's Smock Alley Theatre, drawing over 1,100 entries.

## ⚖️ Core Rules & Eligibility

* **Amateur Status Only:** The competition is strictly open to amateur photographers. An amateur is defined as someone who derives **no more than 20% of their total income** from photography.
* **Geographic Constraint:** All photographs must be taken within the **Republic of Ireland**.
* **Authenticity & No Digital Manipulation:** Clean Coasts enforces strict editing rules. Standard light and tone adjustments (such as contrast, exposure, white balance, and sharpening) are fully permitted. However, cloning out backscatter dots, removing environmental elements, adding objects, or utilizing any generative AI modifications will result in immediate disqualification.

## 🏆 Competitive Categories

The competition features five main categories covering various coastal themes:

1. **Underwater:** Focuses on marine and freshwater life beneath the surface. Excellent for specialized macro or wide-angle setups highlighting hidden native biodiversity.
2. **Wildlife and the Coast:** Captures coastal birds, mammals, insects, and marine life interactions above or at the water's surface.
3. **Coastal Landscape:** Showcases the raw beauty, geology, and dramatic scenery of Ireland's shoreline.
4. **People and the Coast:** Highlights human connection, activities, sports, culture, and heritage along the coast.
5. **Source to Sea:** Focuses on inland waterways, streams, rivers, and estuaries, tracking their journey and connection down to the open ocean.

## 💡 Submission Formatting & Technical Requirements

Entries must be submitted through the official Clean Coasts online portal according to strict file and metadata rules:

* **Platform:** Clean Coasts Online Submission Portal.
* **File Naming Format:** Image files must be explicitly renamed prior to upload using the following exact structure:
  `Photographer Name_Title of Photograph_Location of Photograph.jpg`
* **Metadata Requirements:** The submission form requires manual entry of detailed technical metadata for each shot, including:
  * Camera body model
  * Lens model / focal length
  * Shutter speed
  * Aperture ($f$-stop)
  * ISO speed
  * Any specialized lighting gear used (e.g., strobes, diffusers, optical snoots)

## 🪼 Wildlife Ethics & Environmental Compliance

* **The "Ethics" Guide:** Clean Coasts issues an official photography guide outlining strict ethical practices regarding coastal wildlife. Downloading, reviewing, and adhering to these wildlife guidelines is a mandatory component of entering the competition.
* **Disturbance:** Photographers must never disrupt, distress, or alter the natural behavior of marine or terrestrial wildlife to capture an image.
