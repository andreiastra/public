## Irish Wildlife Trust — Photo of the Month

**Irish Wildlife Trust (IWT)**  
🔗 [facebook.com/IrishWildlifeTrust](https://www.facebook.com/IrishWildlifeTrust/)

### Overview
A popular monthly photography competition celebrating native Irish biodiversity, run via the IWT Facebook community. Selected entries progress to a public voting album on Facebook.

### Eligibility
- Open to all photographers.
- Subject must be native Irish wildlife.

### Submission
Send an email to **photocomp@iwt.ie** with:

| Required Field | Detail |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Subject line** | Must read exactly `Photo of the Month` |
| **Date of capture** | Format: dd/mm/yyyy — required for every photo attached |
| **Location** | At minimum, the county of capture for every photo |
| **Images** | Multiple images may be attached; the panel selects a maximum of **one per person** for the voting album |

📁 [`Irish-Wildlife-Trust/`](Irish-Wildlife-Trust/)

## Nature Network Ireland — Monthly Competition

**Nature Network Ireland**  
🔗 [facebook.com/naturenetworkireland](https://www.facebook.com/naturenetworkireland)  
📧 [competitions@naturenetworkireland.ie](mailto:competitions@naturenetworkireland.ie)

### Overview
A free monthly competition celebrating Ireland's wildlife, habitats, and biodiversity. Open to amateur and advanced photographers. Winners are showcased in community voting rounds on Facebook and Instagram.

### Eligibility
- Open to photographers aged **16 or over**.
- Photos must be taken within **Ireland** (Republic and Northern Ireland).
- Subject must be **native species or habitats** (terrestrial, freshwater, and marine/underwater).
- AI-generated images are **strictly prohibited**.

### Submission Window
| Phase | Date |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Opens** | 8th of each month |
| **Closes** | Last day of each month at **midnight (IST)** |

Archive photos are eligible — images do not need to be taken during the active month.

### How to Submit
Email up to **2 photographs** per month to `competitions@naturenetworkireland.ie` and include:

1. **Exact species name** — common and/or scientific (e.g., *Tompot Blenny*, *Snakelocks Anemone*)
2. **Caption** — brief description or context
3. **Category** — state **Amateur** or **Advanced**
4. **Calendar opt-out** *(optional)* — explicitly request exclusion from the annual charity calendar if preferred
5. **NPWS licence** *(if applicable)* — required for any entry featuring wild nesting birds

### Accepted File Formats
JPEG, HEIF, PNG, DNG (raw)

📁 [`Nature-Network-Ireland/`](Nature-Network-Ireland/)
