# BSoUP Monthly Competitions: Overview & Participation Guide

A comprehensive summary of the monthly underwater photography competitions run by the **British Society of Underwater Photographers (BSoUP)**.

---

## 📌 Overview & Structure

* **Host:** British Society of Underwater Photographers (BSoUP)
* **Website:** [https://bsoup.org.uk/competitions/monthly-competitions/](https://bsoup.org.uk/competitions/monthly-competitions/)
* **Format:** Year-round, themed digital underwater photography competitions running on a monthly calendar.
* **Cadence:** Held once a month in conjunction with BSoUP's monthly online meetings (typically Tuesday evenings via Zoom).
* **Judging & Live Feedback:** Each round is reviewed and critiqued live by invited guest judges—prominent UK and international underwater photographers—providing technical and artistic feedback.
* **Recognition:** Winning and commended images are showcased in the official BSoUP galleries and published in BSoUP's *In Focus* magazine.

---

## 🌍 Eligibility & Ireland Participation

* **Open to Ireland:** Yes. Underwater photographers residing in Ireland can fully participate.
* **100% Online Format:** Submissions, presentations, guest talks, and live judging take place digitally via Zoom, making it completely accessible from Ireland.
* **Membership Requirement:** Unlike standalone open events like the BIUPC (which allows guest entry fees), the **Monthly Competitions are strictly exclusive to active BSoUP members**.
* **Annual Subscription:** 
  * Full Membership: **£35 / year**
  * Student Membership (full-time): **£10 / year**
  * Sign-up is completed online directly through the BSoUP website.

---

## 🎨 Common Monthly Themes & Categories

Each month features a specific rotating theme designed to test different technical setups and creative skills, including:

* **Macro / Super-Macro:** High-magnification close-ups, snooted subjects, and shallow depth of field.
* **Marine Animal Behaviour:** Symbiosis, feeding, cleaning stations, and mating/reproduction.
* **Wide Angle / Reef Scenery:** Kelp forests, reefscapes, and shipwrecks.
* **Animals in their Environment:** Wide/medium framing showcasing the subject alongside its natural habitat.
* **British & Irish Waters:** Celebrating native marine and freshwater biodiversity across the UK and Ireland.
* **Abstract & Natural Artistry:** Textures, patterns, light play, and creative compositions.
* **Theme Portfolios:** Curated 3-image story sequences.

---

## ⚙️ Submission & Workflow Summary

1. **Active Membership:** Maintain an active annual BSoUP membership.
2. **Review Theme & Rules:** Check the monthly schedule on the BSoUP members' area for current theme constraints and deadlines.
3. **Portal Upload:** Submit entries via the BSoUP members' competition portal ahead of the monthly cut-off date.
4. **Join the Live Meeting:** Attend the monthly Zoom meeting to watch live judging, scoring, and panel critiques.
