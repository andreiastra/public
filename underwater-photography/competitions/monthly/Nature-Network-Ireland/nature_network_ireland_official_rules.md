# Nature Network Ireland – Photography Competition Rules & Guidelines

*Help us harness the power of photography to showcase a national love for Ireland’s wildlife and habitats.*

---

## 📌 Overview & Mission

Nature Network Ireland hosts a ongoing monthly photography competition designed to celebrate and document Ireland's rich biodiversity, native wildlife, and natural habitats. The contest caters to both amateur enthusiasts and advanced photographers across Ireland.

- **Official Website / Socials:** [Nature Network Ireland Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/naturenetworkireland)
- **Submission Email:** [competitions@naturenetworkireland.ie](mailto:competitions@naturenetworkireland.ie)
- **Entry Fee:** Completely **FREE** to enter (No purchase necessary)

---

## 📅 Timeline & Monthly Submission Cycle

The competition operates on a predictable monthly schedule throughout the year:

| Event / Phase | Details & Schedule |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Opening Date** | **8th of each month** |
| **Closing Date** | **Last day of each month at Midnight (12:00 AM IST)** |
| **Photo Capture Date** | Archive photos are fully eligible; photos do **not** need to be taken during the active month. |
| **Voting & Announcements** | Monthly category winners (Amateur & Advanced) are showcased for public voting on social media (Facebook & Instagram) and notified via email. |

---

## ⚖️ Core Rules & Eligibility

1. **Age & Experience:**
   - Open to all photographers aged **16 or over**.
   - Categories are split into **Amateur** and **Advanced** tracks to ensure fair competition across skill levels.

2. **Geographic Scope:**
   - All photographs **must be taken within Ireland** ( Republic of Ireland and Northern Ireland / island of Ireland).

3. **Subject Matter Rules:**
   - Entries are strictly restricted to **native species and habitats** (including terrestrial, freshwater, and marine/underwater environments).
   - Non-native wild species living in Ireland may be permitted strictly at Nature Network Ireland's discretion.

4. **Authenticity & Originality:**
   - All imagery **MUST be the exclusive original work** of the submitting photographer.
   - Images must not contain any element copyrighted by another person.
   - **AI-generated images are strictly prohibited.**

5. **Submission Limits:**
   - Photographers may submit multiple entries per month.
   - Each individual photographer is capped at a **maximum of two (2) photographs**.

---

## ⚙️ Technical Specifications & Email Formatting

### Supported Formats & Equipment
- **Accepted File Types:** JPEG, HEIF, PNG, and raw DNG files.
- **Equipment:** Any device capable of capturing digital images of sufficient quality for digital display is accepted (including smartphones, tablets, DSLRs, mirrorless systems, and underwater housings).

### Mandatory Submission Email Requirements
When sending entries to `competitions@naturenetworkireland.ie`, your email **must** include:

1. **Exact Species Name:** State the common and/or scientific name (e.g., *Tompot Blenny*, *European Robin*, *Snakelocks Anemone*).
2. **Caption:** A brief description or context for the image.
3. **Category Track:** State clearly whether you are entering as **Amateur** or **Advanced**.
4. **Calendar Opt-out (Optional):** If you do **not** want your image considered for inclusion in the annual charity calendar, explicitly state this in the body text of your email.
5. **NPWS Licence (If Applicable):** Required if your entry features wild nesting birds (see ethics below).

---

## 🐍 Wildlife Ethics, Legal Compliance & Nesting Birds

- **Nesting Birds Policy:**
  - In accordance with Irish and European Union law, disturbing or photographing wild nesting birds on or near their breeding place requires an official licence from the **National Parks and Wildlife Service (NPWS)**.
  - If your submission includes photographs of birds at or near their nest, you **must attach evidence or a copy of your NPWS licence** with your entry.
  - Information & Licence Application: [NPWS Wildlife Licences](https://www.npws.ie/licencesandconsents/education-and-science/photograph-or-film-protected-wild-animal-or-bird)
- **General Welfare:**
  - Photographers must prioritize animal welfare, avoiding disruption or distress to native fauna and delicate habitats (both on land and underwater).

## 📝 Rights, Usage & Community Voting

- **Promotional Rights:** By entering, you grant Nature Network Ireland non-exclusive consent to display your submitted photograph(s) on their official website, social media channels (specifically Facebook and Instagram for public voting), and promotional materials.
- **Photographer Credit:** Full attribution and credit will **always** be aligned with the photographer whenever the image is displayed or published.
- **Annual Charity Calendar:** Submitted images may be selected for inclusion in the annual Nature Network Ireland charity calendar, unless the photographer explicitly opts out in the entry email.
- **Disqualification Right:** Nature Network Ireland reserves the right, at its sole discretion, to disregard any entry that fails to comply with the rules or the spirit of the competition.
