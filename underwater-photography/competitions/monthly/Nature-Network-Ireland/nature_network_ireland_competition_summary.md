# Nature Network Ireland – Monthly Photography Competition Summary

A comprehensive overview of the rules, timelines, and technical requirements for submitting native wildlife and habitat photography.
https://www.facebook.com/naturenetworkireland

## 📅 Timeline & Submission Windows
The competition runs on a strict monthly cycle. The dates below dictate the **submission window** only; photos do **not** need to be captured during the active month.
* **Opening Date:** The 8th of each month.
* **Closing Date:** The last day of each month at **midnight**.

## ⚖️ Core Rules & Eligibility
* **Skill Categories:** Split into **Amateur** and **Advanced** categories.
* **Geographic Constraint:** All photographs must be taken within **Ireland**.
* **Subject Matter:** Restricted to **native species and habitats** only. This fully includes **underwater marine and freshwater environments** (e.g., native fish, invertebrates, anemones, kelp forests, or mammals).
* **Authenticity:** Images must be your own original work. **AI-generated images are strictly prohibited.**
* **Submission Limit:** Multiple entries are permitted per month, but with a **maximum of two photographs per individual email submission**.

## ⚙️ Technical Specifications & Formats
* **Supported Formats:** JPEG, HEIF, PNG, and raw DNG files.
* **Equipment:** Photos can be shot on any digital device (including DSLRs, mirrorless cameras, underwater housings, smartphones, or tablets) provided the resolution is of sufficient quality for digital display.

## 📧 How to Submit
Send your email entry directly to: **`competitions@naturenetworkireland.ie`**
Just to let you know we will from August onwards we are only taking submissions to this email (competitions@naturenetworkireland.ie)! 

### Mandatory Email Details:
1. **Exact Species Name:** (e.g., *Eurasian Otter*, *European Robin*, *Tompot Blenny*).
2. **Caption:** A brief caption or description for the photograph.
3. **Category Preference:** Indicate if you are entering the Amateur or Advanced track.
4. **Calendar Opt-out (Optional):** If you do **not** want your image considered for the annual charity calendar, you must explicitly state this in the text of your email.

## 🐍 Wildlife Ethics & Legal Compliance
* **Nesting Birds:** In accordance with Irish and EU law, photographing wild nesting birds on or near their breeding place requires a licence from the National Parks and Wildlife Service (NPWS). If your entry features a bird at or near its nest, you **must attach a copy or evidence of your NPWS licence** with your submission.
* **Disturbance:** General wildlife photography should prioritize the welfare of the animal, avoiding disruption to natural behaviors or habitats (both terrestrial and marine).

## 📝 Rights & Community Voting
* **Usage:** By entering, you grant Nature Network Ireland consent to display your photograph on their website, social media channels for community voting rounds, and promotional material.
* **Credit:** The photographer will always receive **full attribution and credit** whenever the image is used.
