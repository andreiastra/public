# Competition Overview & Email Guidelines

The Irish Wildlife Trust (IWT) runs a popular monthly photography competition via its Facebook community to celebrate native Irish biodiversity. To ensure your entries are successfully processed by the selection panel, the submission email must adhere strictly to the following parameters:

* **Submission Address:** All entries must be emailed directly to photocomp@iwt.ie.
* **Subject Line Rule:** The email subject line must read exactly `"Photo of the Month"`—omitting this or changing the phrasing can cause the entry to be missed by automated inbox filters.
* **Required Image Metadata:** The body of the email must explicitly list the date of capture (dd/mm/yyyy) and at least the county location for every single attached photograph.
* **Selection Limit:** Photographers are permitted to attach multiple images in a single email to give the panel options. However, the curation team will select a maximum of one photograph per person to progress to the final public voting album on Facebook.
