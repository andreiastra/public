## 🏆 Detailed Competition Breakdown

### 1. 🌊 BIUPC 2026 (British & Irish Underwater Photography Championship)
* **Live Shoot-Out Date:** Saturday, 22 August 2026
* **Shooting Window:** From 10:00 PM on Friday, August 21st until 10:00 PM on Saturday, August 22nd, 2026.
* **The Unique Authentication Rule:** To guarantee files are shot live, the British Society of Underwater Photographers (BSoUP) will release a verification code/image at 8:00 PM on Friday, August 21st. This code must be registered inside your camera configuration according to their official guidelines.
* **Environment Rules:** Open to fresh and saltwater environments across the UK and Ireland. All entries must be taken completely submerged.
* **Key Targets for West Cork:** Ideal for close-up macro setups around Lough Hyne during optimal tidal cycles. Focus on categories like *Close Up (Open)* or *Human Impact*.

### 2. 🏛️ Water Heritage Day 2026 (Heritage Council & LAWPRO)
* **Live Focus Day:** Sunday, August 23, 2026
* **2026 Core Theme:** **"Heritage at Risk"**
* **The Challenge:** Document how local coastal regions, marine reserves, rivers, or lakes face environmental or climate shifts.
* **Strategy:** Plan an intensive dive or field day on August 23rd at sensitive ecosystems like Lough Hyne or Tragumna shallows. Frame fragile native species, reproductive cycles, or threatened habitats (such as seagrass beds) to align with the core criteria.

### 3. 🆕 IPF National Nature & Wildlife Competition (2026)
* **Submission Window:** Opens Wednesday, August 19, 2026 (9:00 AM) | Closes Wednesday, September 9, 2026 (12:00 AM).
* **Technical Constraints:** Operates under strict FIAP rules. Absolutely no digital cloning, healing backscatter, or composite imagery is allowed. 
* **Focus:** High-level proof of technical mastery and native biological accuracy. Keep original EXIF data intact.

### 4. 📝 The Brian Black Memorial Award 2026
* **Submission Deadline:** August 30, 2026
* **The Core Requirement:** A photojournalism award focusing on "Our Inspiring Seas". Entries must track the environmental narrative of a single voyage, specific cruise, or an intensive single-day exploration documenting threats like climate change or ghost fishing gear.
* **Prizes:** £3,000 total prize pool, print publication in *Yachting Monthly*, and equipment. Free to enter.

### 5. 📧 Rolling Monthly Tracks (August Rounds)

#### **Nature Network Ireland**
* **Window:** August 8th – August 31st, 2026 (Midnight)
* **Where to Send:** `competitions@naturenetworkireland.ie`
* **Rules:** Maximum 2 photographs per individual email. Must state species name, caption, and track (*Amateur* or *Advanced*). Supports raw DNG file submittals.

#### **Irish Wildlife Trust (IWT) Photo of the Month**
* **Window:** Ongoing monthly cycle.
* **Where to Send:** `photocomp@iwt.ie`
* **Strict Subject Line Rule:** Must be exactly `Photo of the Month`.
* **Details:** Include date of capture and the county location in the email body. One selected photo per person moves to the public Facebook voting album.
