# Comprehensive Underwater & Wildlife Photography Competitions Guide

A master technical and operational guide covering deadlines, submission procedures, rules, and technical specifications for major UK and Ireland photography competitions.

---

## 🏆 British & Irish Underwater Photography Championship (BIUPC)

A live, 24-hour "shoot-out" competition organized by the British Society of Underwater Photographers (BSoUP).

### 📅 Critical Timelines
* **Verification Image Release:** Friday, August 21, 2026, at ~8:00 PM (published on the BSoUP website and Facebook page).
* **Shooting & Submission Window:** 10:00 PM on Friday, August 21, 2026, to 10:00 PM on Saturday, August 22, 2026.
* **Submission Deadline:** All files must be uploaded before **10:00 PM on Saturday, August 22, 2026**.
* **Results Announcement:** Broadcast live 2–3 weeks following the competition at the BSoUP monthly meeting.

### 🌊 Eligibility & Geographic Boundaries
* **Locations:** Open to any salt or freshwater body (seas, rivers, streams, lakes, and loughs) across England, Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland, the Republic of Ireland, and surrounding islands.
* **Eligibility:** Open to underwater photographers of all nationalities.
* **Entry Fee:** £10.00 GBP (paid via Stripe/PayPal on the BSoUP website).

### ⚙️ Camera Setup & Verification Workflow
1. **Synchronize Clock:** Ensure the camera's internal clock/calendar is set accurately before diving.
2. **RAW Verification Capture:** Display the official verification code/image on a screen/phone and take a **RAW photo** of it with your camera before shooting competition entries.
3. **Archive Verification RAW:** Keep the original RAW verification frame on the memory card (shortlisted entrants must supply this alongside native RAW files).

### 📐 Image Sizing & Export Specifications
* **File Format:** High-quality JPEG (`.jpg`).
* **Pixel Dimensions:** Exactly **3000 pixels** on the longest dimension (width for landscape, height for portrait).
* **Metadata Resolution:** 300 DPI / PPI.
* **Color Space:** sRGB.
* **Watermarks & Borders:** Strictly prohibited.

### 🏷️ Mandatory File Naming Convention
Images must follow this exact structure prior to upload:
```text
Firstname_Lastname_Category_ImageNumber.jpg

CU: Close Up / Macro

WA: Wide Angle

MK: Mankind’s Impact

Examples: Andre_Astrakharchik_CU_1.jpg, Andre_Astrakharchik_WA_1.jpg

⚖️ Permitted vs. Prohibited Editing Rules
Shortlisted entries are audited directly against original, unedited camera RAW files.

✅ Permitted (Global Adjustments Only):

Global exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows, whites, and blacks.

Global color balance, temperature, saturation, and vibrance.

Global Texture, Clarity, Dehaze, and Sharpening across the full frame.

Standard cropping and geometric framing.

❌ Strictly Prohibited (Zero Tolerance):

No Backscatter Removal: Any form of cloning or spot healing to remove backscatter particles, specks, or sensor dust.

No Object Manipulation: Adding, moving, or deleting organisms or background elements.

No Local Adjustments: Selective sharpening, localized blurring, adjustment brushes, radial/linear gradients, or artificial vignettes.

🚀 Submission Process
Upload final exported .jpg files directly to the official BIUPC Dropbox link provided upon registration before the 10:00 PM cutoff on Saturday, August 22.

Retain all native RAW files and the initial verification RAW file for finalist verification.
