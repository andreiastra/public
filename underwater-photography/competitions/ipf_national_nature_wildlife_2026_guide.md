## ⚖️ Strict Technical Regulations & FIAP Rules
The IPF National Nature & Wildlife Competition enforces elite technical rules modeled directly after international **FIAP Nature definitions**. For underwater photographers, this presents both a challenge and a massive strategic opportunity:

* **The Absolute Non-Manipulation Clause:** Digitally cloning, healing, replacing backgrounds, or manipulating pixels is strictly prohibited. You **cannot** clone out backscatter, lighting artifacts, or floaters in post-processing.
* **Biological Reality:** Images must completely reflect the actual biological accuracy and environmental reality at the moment of capture.
* **EXIF Integrity:** Original metadata (including camera model, lens parameters, shutter speed, aperture, and capture date) must remain fully intact within the file structure.

## 🌊 Underwater Macro Mapping & Judging Tracks
Your specialized West Cork marine macro and wildlife archives can be directly mapped into the following core categories:

### 1. Fauna Track (Prints / Digital)
* **Target Subjects:** Fine-art portraits of native Irish fish species and active marine vertebrates.
* **Strategic Selection:** Crisp profiles of species like the *Tompot Blenny* (*Parablennius gattorugine*) or localized *Lumpsuckers*.

### 2. Macro Track (Prints)
* **Target Subjects:** High-magnification close-up documentation of native marine invertebrates and fragile structures.
* **Strategic Selection:** Detailed close-ups of *Nudibranchs*, skeleton shrimp, or the intricate geometry of individual cup coral polyps.

### 3. Animal Interactions (Projected Digital Images - PDI)
* **Target Subjects:** Images capturing authentic wildlife behavior or biological relationships without staging.
* **Strategic Selection:** Symbiotic relationships from local shore diving zones, such as an *Inachus spider crab* or ghost shrimp perfectly nestled within the protective tentacles of a *Snakelocks Anemone*.

### 4. Our Living Coast (PDI)
* **Target Subjects:** Capturing native species in direct relation to their immediate intertidal or shoreline environment.
* **Strategic Selection:** Marine life interacting with native kelp forests or tidal reef structures along the coastal shelves of West Cork.

## 📸 In-Camera Execution Strategy (West Cork Archive Polish)
Since digital removal of backscatter is explicitly banned under FIAP criteria, your specialized gear setup is your greatest asset:

* **Strobe Positioning:** Ensure your strobes are pushed far out and angled slightly away from the camera axis to prevent lighting up particles between the port and the subject.
* **Optical Snoot Utilization:** Utilize your optical snoot to focus a narrow, isolated beam of light directly onto the primary subject. This naturally drops the background into solid black and prevents ambient backscatter from ever entering the frame, delivering a clean, high-contrast, winning file directly in-camera.
