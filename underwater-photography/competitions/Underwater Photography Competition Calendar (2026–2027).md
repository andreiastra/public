# August 2026

## British & Irish Underwater Photography Championship (BIUPC)

**Competition Date:** 22 August 2026

**Website**
https://www.britishunderwaterimagefestival.co.uk/

### Format

- One-day Shoot-Out competition
- All images must be captured on the competition day
- Open to photographers diving in the UK and Ireland
- Marine and freshwater locations are eligible

### Dive Plan

**Location:** Lough Hyne, Ireland

### Primary Subjects

- Nudibranchs
- Shrimps
- Gobies
- Sea anemones
- Marine behaviour
- Creative snoot lighting
- Unique compositions

### Goal

Avoid creating simple species portraits.

Look for:

- Behaviour
- Interaction
- Feeding
- Egg laying
- Symbiosis
- Storytelling

# Post-Dive Workflow

After every dive:

- Backup RAW files
- Create a second backup
- Rate images
- Flag competition candidates
- Record species identification
- Record dive location
- Record lens and settings
- Perform initial editing
- Export previews
- Move the strongest images into a dedicated **Competition Portfolio** folder
