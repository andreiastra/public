# Depth of Field vs Aperture — M.ZUIKO DIGITAL ED 8mm f/1.8 Fisheye PRO

## Setup

- Camera: OM SYSTEM OM-1 Mark II
- Lens: M.ZUIKO DIGITAL ED 8mm f/1.8 Fisheye PRO
- Sensor: Micro Four Thirds
- Focus distance used for estimates: 0.5 m

Because the 8mm fisheye is extremely wide, depth of field becomes very large very quickly.

## Practical Underwater Recommendations

### f/5.6–f/8
- Best balance of sharpness and depth of field
- Typical close-focus wide-angle underwater range

### f/11
- Useful when maximum foreground/background sharpness is needed
- Slight diffraction begins on Micro Four Thirds

### f/16–f/22
- Significant diffraction softening on OM-1 sensor
- Usually avoided unless absolutely necessary
