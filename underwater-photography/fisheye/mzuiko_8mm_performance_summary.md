# M.Zuiko Digital ED 8mm f/1.8 Fisheye PRO: Performance Profile

This document outlines the optical performance characteristics, landmark labels, and depth of field (DOF) metrics for the **M.ZUIKO DIGITAL ED 8mm f/1.8 Fisheye PRO** lens on the Micro Four Thirds (M4F) system. 

Unlike macro lenses, an ultra-wide 8mm fisheye optic possesses an inherently massive depth of field even at wider apertures, with its absolute edge-to-edge sharpness peaking around f/5.6 before hitting sensor-level diffraction limitations.

---

## Key Technical Insights for Field Use

1. **The Diffraction Threshold:** For Micro Four Thirds sensors, the practical limit for high-resolution work lands at **f/8.0 to f/11.0**. Exceeding f/11.0 results in severe image degradation due to the physical limitations of light passing through small apertures.
2. **Fisheye Depth of Field:** Because of the ultra-wide 8mm focal length, almost everything from a few feet to infinity is in sharp focus by f/4.0 or f/5.6. The relative depth of field curve accelerates rapidly compared to standard or macro lenses.
3. **Corner Performance:** While the center is sharp even at f/1.8, stopping down to f/4.0 or f/5.6 is highly recommended if your composition relies on sharp details along the extreme edges of the 180-degree field of view.
