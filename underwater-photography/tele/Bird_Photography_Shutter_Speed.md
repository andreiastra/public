# 🦅 Bird Photography Shutter Speed Reference Guide

A field and technical shutter speed reference for the **OM SYSTEM OM-1 Mark II** paired with the **M.Zuiko Digital ED 100-400mm f/5.0–6.3 IS** lens (effective full-frame equivalent reach: 200–800mm)[cite: 3, 5, 9].

---

## 📋 Shutter Speed Reference Table

| Subject / Bird Behavior | Recommended Shutter Speed | Purpose & Technical Considerations |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stationary / Perched / Roosting**<br>*(Sleeping shorebirds, large perched herons)* | **1/250s – 1/500s** | Keeps ISO at base (ISO 200) for maximum resolution and dynamic range; relies on lens & body IS for handheld stabilization[cite: 5, 6]. |
| **Active Perched / Small Songbirds**<br>*(Twitching heads, hopping on branches, foraging)* | **1/800s – 1/1250s** | Freezes rapid micro-movements of heads and tails that IS cannot counteract. |
| **Slow Flight / Gliding / Large Birds**<br>*(Herons, swans, buzzards, soaring gulls)* | **1/1600s – 1/2000s**[cite: 5] | Freezes broad wingbeats and fast linear movement across the frame[cite: 5]. |
| **Fast Flight / Small Birds in Flight (BIF)**<br>*(Swallows, ducks, kingfishers, diving terns)* | **1/2500s – 1/3200s** | **The gold standard for BIF.** Freezes high-speed wing tips and sudden erratic turns. |
| **Artistic Motion Blur / Panning**<br>*(Tracking flying birds along the water/shore)* | **1/30s – 1/125s**[cite: 5] | Blurs background and wingtips while keeping the bird's eye and head sharp through smooth tracking. |

---

## ⚙️ Core Camera & Lens Rules (OM-1 Mark II + 100-400mm)

1. **Exposure Mode:** **Manual (M) + Auto ISO**[cite: 3]  
   Set your shutter speed to match the behavior above, lock aperture to the lens sweet spot (**f/6.3** at 100–300mm or **f/8.0** at 400mm)[cite: 5], and allow Auto ISO to float up to **ISO 6400**[cite: 3].
2. **The 800mm Reach Factor:**  
   Because 400mm on Micro Four Thirds equals an **800mm full-frame field of view**[cite: 5, 8], motion across the sensor is heavily magnified[cite: 8]. When in doubt, err on the faster side (e.g., **1/2500s**) rather than risking soft feather detail.
3. **Autofocus & AI Tracking:**[cite: 3]  
   * Focus Mode: **Continuous AF (C-AF)**[cite: 3]
   * Subject Detection: **Birds**[cite: 3]
4. **Hardware Focus Limiter:**  
   Set the lens barrel switch to **`6m – ∞`** to prevent focus hunting through the macro range when tracking birds against complex backgrounds.
