## 🔬 Core Optical Principles

### 1. Aperture Sweet Spot (f/6.3 – f/8.0)
* **Peak Range:** Across all focal lengths ($100\text{--}400\text{mm}$), stopping down $1/3$ to $1$ full stop to **f/6.3 – f/8.0** maximizes contrast and resolving power[cite: 4].
* **Long End (400mm):** At $400\text{mm}$ wide open (f/6.3), contrast dips slightly; stopping down to **f/8.0** sharpens micro-details (feather and fur textures)[cite: 4].
* **Diffraction Limit:** On Micro Four Thirds sensors (20.4MP Live MOS on OM-1 II), diffraction softens fine details beyond **f/8.0**[cite: 2, 9]. Avoid **f/11, f/16, or f/22**.

### 2. Focal Length Performance
* **100mm – 300mm:** Exceptional resolution, corner-to-corner clarity, and punchy contrast[cite: 2].
* **350mm – 400mm:** Backing off slightly to **~350mm** or stopping down to **f/8.0 at 400mm** preserves optimal clarity[cite: 2, 4].

## ⚙️ Recommended OM-1 Mark II Field Settings

### 1. Avian & Wildlife Telephoto (Custom Mode C3)
* **Mode:** Manual (M) with Auto ISO (up to ISO 6400)[cite: 3].
* **Aperture:** `f/6.3` (100–300mm) or `f/8.0` (300–400mm)[cite: 4].
* **Shutter Speeds:**
  * *Birds in Flight (BIF) / Action:* `1/2000s – 1/3200s` to freeze wing motion at 800mm equivalent reach[cite: 2].
  * *Perched Birds / Static Wildlife:* `1/500s – 1/1000s`[cite: 2].
* **AF Target & AI:** Bird AI Subject Detection AF (`C-AF`)[cite: 3].

### 2. Solar & Eclipse Observation (DIY Solarix Collar)
* **Optics & Filtration:** 100-400mm with DIY Explore Scientific Solarix A4 Film Collar mounted over the lens hood[cite: 4, 9].
* **Exposure:** Manual (M), `1/500s – 1/2000s`, `f/8.0`, `ISO 100/200`[cite: 4].
* **Focus Mode:** `S-AF+MF` (Lock crescent edge with Single AF, fine-tune/hold with Manual Focus)[cite: 4].
* **Support:** Manfrotto 055PROB Tripod + 486RC2 Ball Head with 2-sec Self-Timer[cite: 2, 4, 9].

### 3. Hardware Barrel Controls
* **Focus Limiter Switch:**
  * `6m – ∞`: **Recommended default** for birds and distant wildlife to prevent focus hunting through macro range.
  * `1.3m – 6m`: Tele-macro for insects, dragonflies, and small reptiles.
  * `FULL`: Use when subject distance varies wildly.
* **Lens IS Switch:** Keep **ON**. Coordinates with OM-1 II body stabilization[cite: 2, 9].
