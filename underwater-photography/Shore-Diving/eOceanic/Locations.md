# eOceanic Cork locations

In eOceanic's order, which follows the coast clockwise from Youghal to Ardgroom, so roughly east to west. It is not strictly sorted by longitude: the list follows the shoreline, so it doubles back where the coast does, such as up Bantry Bay. Generated from [`eoceanic_cork_sites.json`](eoceanic_cork_sites.json) by `scripts/extract_eoceanic.py`; edit the script, not this file.

The zones are my own grouping, not eOceanic's, which has none. Each is one unbroken stretch of the coast in eOceanic's order, so places on the same bay stay together, and where one ends and the next begins is a judgement call. They are geographic only, say nothing about swell, shelter or dive conditions, and are not the 4 drive-time zones in the [parent README](../README.md#zones). The boundaries are the `ZONES` list in the script.

Positions are eOceanic's haven position, which can be some distance from where a diver would enter the water. Each position links to Google Maps. Depth is at the anchorage at low water springs. See the [README](README.md).

## Ratings

Shelter and Approaches are eOceanic's 1-5 ratings, written for a boat at anchor, not a shore diver. Higher is better.

| Rating | Shelter: how well protected the anchorage is | Approaches: how hard the entrance is to navigate |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 5 | Complete protection: all-round shelter in all reasonable conditions | Safe access: all reasonable conditions |
| 🟡 4 | Good: assured night's sleep except from specific quarters | Straightforward: no overly complex dangers when unaffected by weather from difficult quadrants or tidal consideration |
| 🟠 3 | Tolerable: in suitable conditions a vessel may be left unwatched for an overnight stay | Attentive navigation: daylight access, with dangers that need attention |
| 🔴 2 | Exposed: unattended vessels should be watched from the shore, and a comfortable overnight stay is unlikely | Careful navigation: good visibility and conditions needed, with dangers that require careful navigation |
| ⚫ 1 | Stay-aboard: lunch stop or tide-wait only, exposed or tenuous holding, vessel should not be left unattended | Highly involved navigation: complex dangers that require arduous navigation, good visibility and settled conditions |
