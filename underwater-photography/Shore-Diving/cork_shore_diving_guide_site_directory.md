# Cork Shore Diving Master Directory

This directory is built directly from your primary dataset **`Cork_Shore_Diving.csv`** (56 sites in original list order). Each site is supplemented with:
* **Coordinates:** Verified decimal latitude/longitude derived via Google Maps.
* **eOceanic Pilot Intelligence:** Shelter ratings (1★ to 5★), charted depths at Low Water Springs (LWS), substrate, and tidal/swell hazards.
* **Diving Ireland (CFT) Data:** Cited *only* where it provides added operational value (such as direct links to CFT Emergency Response Plans, official dive guides, or backup launch ramps).

---

### Shelter Rating Legend (eOceanic)
* 🟢 **5★ (Complete):** Total protection from Atlantic swells in all reasonable conditions. Top adverse-weather fallback.
* 🟡 **4★ (Good):** Reliable protection; sheltered except from specific opposing wind quarters.
* 🟠 **3★ (Tolerable):** Diveable in settled conditions; watch for residual swell or surge.
* 🔴 **2★ (Exposed):** Open to Atlantic groundswells; requires flat calm seas and light offshore winds.
* ⚫ **1★ (Stay-aboard / Surf):** Heavy open Atlantic roadstead or surf beach; rare summer windows only.
