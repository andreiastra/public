**What was different about how it ran**
- **One download instead of many.** Running the same scripts one by one needs 21 paginated requests: 3 for `fetch_observations.py`, 3 each for `generate_location_ratings.py`, `underwater_ireland.py`, `site_observations.py` and the two rarity scripts, 2 for `species_classification.py` and 1 for `nudibranch_ireland.py`. The run made 3.
- **Filtering done locally.** The Ireland and nudibranch subsets come from the downloaded list. Before the change I checked that this gives the same observations as the API filters (Ireland 308, nudibranchs 15).
- **Snapshot moved.** `observations.json` is now in `scripts/.cache/`, not `reports/`.
- **Time is now rarity calls.** The two rarity reports make 2 API calls for each of 79 species, 316 calls in all. They take 358 of the 367 seconds, and nothing can replace those calls.

### The main result: 13 underwater observations became Research Grade

**Thirteen underwater observations moved from Needs ID to Research Grade.** This is the most important change in the run. The underwater total did not change (208), but the grades did.

It matters because the rarity reports count Research Grade only. These upgrades are why `rarity_underwater_ireland.md` and `rarity_underwater_worldwide.md` grew from 74 to 79 taxa (see below).

Nine are from the Audley Cove dive on 2026-11-07:
Sand Fan Worm, Common Hermit Crab, Black Goby, Two-spotted Goby (two observations), Velvet Swimming Crab, Common Dragonet, Greater Pipefish, Pimplet Anemone.

Overall Research Grade went from 209 to 232 (13 upgrades plus the 10 home birds below), and Needs ID fell from 89 to 76 (the 13 upgrades).
