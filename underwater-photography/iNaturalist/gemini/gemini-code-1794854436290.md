## 2. Flagship Scientific & Conservation Highlights

* **Inter-Polychaete Predation (`Obs #395271306`):** Football Jersey Worm (*Tubulanus annulatus*) actively extracting a fan worm (*Sabellidae*) from its tube—high-value trophic data for **GloBI**.
* **Global Platform Rarities:**
  * **Lobe Shell (*Philine quadripartita*):** Sand-dwelling bubble snail in Lough Hyne (`Obs #396151006`, `#395268141`; ~143 global iNaturalist records).
  * **Pimplet Anemone (*Anthopleura ballii*):** Logged at 5 m in Lough Hyne (`Obs #396151001`; ~513 global records).
  * **Connemarra Clingfish (*Lepadogaster candolii*):** Cryptic crevice-dwelling fish at Zetland Pier (`Obs #395276891`).
* **Seasearch-Aligned Subtidal Metadata:** Lough Hyne series (*Inachus phalangium*, *Necora puber*, *Gobius niger*) includes quantitative **Depth (`5 m`)** and **Water Temperature (`18°C`)** fields, directly supporting **NBDC** and **Seasearch Ireland** biotope mapping.
