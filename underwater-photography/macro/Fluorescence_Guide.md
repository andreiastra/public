# Bio-Fluorescence Macro Photography Guide

A dedicated reference guide focused exclusively on bio-fluorescence photography for both land (countryside evening/night) and underwater (West Cork shore diving) using high-power strobe excitation.

---

## 1. The Dual-Filter System (How It Works)

Bio-fluorescence occurs when an organism absorbs high-energy light (UV / Royal Blue) and re-emits it as a lower-energy visible glow (vivid greens, yellows, reds, and magentas). Capturing this requires a complementary dual-filter setup:

$$\text{HF-1 White Flash} \xrightarrow{\text{Strobe Excitation Filter}} \text{Royal Blue / UV Light} \xrightarrow{\text{Subject Glow}} \text{Fluoresced Light} \xrightarrow{\text{Yellow Barrier Filter (Blocks Blue)}} \text{Sensor (Clean Image)}$$

* **1. Strobe Excitation Filter:** Mounts directly to the front of your flash. It blocks visible white light and passes only royal blue/UV wavelengths ($\sim395\text{nm--}450\text{nm}$) to excite the subject.
* **2. Camera Lens Barrier Filter:** Mounts onto your camera lens or housing port. It **blocks the reflected blue excitation light splash** while allowing only the glowing fluoresced wavelengths to reach the camera sensor.

## 3. Recommended Camera & Exposure Settings

High-power flash excitation from the **Backscatter HF-1** delivers massive light energy, allowing crisp, freeze-motion exposures without needing high ISOs or long exposures:

* **Mode:** Manual Mode
* **Shutter Speed:** $1/250\text{s}$ (freezes subject movement and eliminates ambient light)
* **Aperture:** $\text{f}/8\text{--}\text{f}/11$ (ensures sharp depth-of-field)
* **ISO:** 100–200
* **White Balance:** Auto or Custom (the yellow barrier filter handles color correction natively)

## 4. In-Field Quick-Switch Workflow

Using the **AOI M67 Flip Adapter**, you can quickly toggle between standard white-light macro and bio-fluorescence shooting mid-dive or mid-session:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                         BIO-FLUORESCENCE MACRO                          │
│  • HF-1 Strobe: Fluorescence Excitation Filter ATTACHED (Bayonet)      │
│  • Lens / Port: 67mm Yellow Barrier Filter Flipped IN (Over Port)       │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                                    │
                                    ▼
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        STANDARD WHITE-LIGHT MACRO                       │
│  • HF-1 Strobe: Excitation Filter REMOVED (Use Flat/Dome Diffuser)      │
│  • Lens / Port: 67mm Yellow Barrier Filter Flipped OUT                  │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

> **Important:** Always remove the excitation filter from your strobe when flipping the lens barrier filter away; otherwise, the strobe will flood your scene with an overwhelming blue/violet cast.

## 5. Target Bio-Fluorescent Subjects

### Underwater (West Cork Coastline & Shore Dives)
* **Snakelocks Anemones (*Anemonia viridis*):** Bright emerald green glowing tentacles, frequently tipped with vibrant magenta/pink fluorescence.
* **Beadlet Anemones (*Actinia equina*):** Electric blue catch-tentacles (*acrorhagi*) glowing brightly around the base.
* **Juvenile Lumpfish (*Cyclopterus lumpus*):** Intense green bio-fluorescence along their dorsal ridges and skin tubercles.
* **Crustaceans:** Skeleton shrimp, decorator crabs, and squat lobsters displaying amber or emerald glowing carapaces.
* **Marine Flora:** Encrusting coralline algae and bryozoans reacting with deep red or bright green hues.

### Countryside Evening & Night (Land Macro)
* **Insects & Arachnids:** Scorpions, millipedes, harvestmen, and certain caterpillars glowing vivid green under UV light.
* **Fungi & Lichens:** Tree bark lichens, mosses, and mushroom caps displaying neon orange, green, or yellow fluorescence.
