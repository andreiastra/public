# Moon Photography Optimization Guide

A summary of your optimized camera settings for capturing high-detail lunar shots using your micro four-thirds setup.

## Current Setup vs. Target Settings

| Setting | Current Value | Optimized Target Value | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Exposure Mode** | Manual (M) | **Manual (M)** | Gives complete control over exposure without interference from dark background sky. |
| **Metering Mode** | Spot | **Spot** | Tells the camera to measure brightness exactly on the moon's surface, preventing blown-out highlights. |
| **Focal Length** | 374mm | **400mm** | Maximize physical optical reach (749mm equivalent field of view). |
| **Aperture** | f/7.1 | **f/7.1 to f/8** | The sweet spot for the OM 100-400mm II lens to minimize chromatic aberration and maximize sharpness. |
| **Shutter Speed** | 1/250s | **1/400s to 1/500s** | Freezes the movement of the moon and mitigates atmospheric turbulence/air jitter. |
| **ISO** | 160 | **200 to 400** | Compensates for the faster shutter speed while keeping noise minimal on the base sensor. |
| **White Balance** | Auto (AWB) | **Daylight (5500K)** | Prevents the camera from incorrectly shifting colors due to surrounding deep blue or black skies. |

## Technical Recommendations for Razor-Sharp Crates

* **Focusing on the Terminator Line:** Switch to Manual Focus (MF) and use the camera’s digital magnification. Target the **terminator line** (the dividing edge between the illuminated and dark side of the moon). The elongated shadows in this region provide the highest contrast, making it easy to fine-tune tack-sharp focus on craters.
* **Eliminate Shutter Shock:** Use the electronic / silent shutter mode or enable a **2-second self-timer delay**. This ensures that physical finger contact with the shutter button doesn't introduce micro-vibrations down your lens barrel.
* **Atmospheric Conditions:** Shoot when the moon is higher in the sky if possible. Lower positions near the horizon force light through a thicker layer of the Earth's atmosphere, increasing heat distortion and softening fine lunar details.
