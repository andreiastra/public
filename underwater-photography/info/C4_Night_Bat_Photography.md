# Custom Mode C4: Night Bat Photography Guide

This document outlines the camera configuration, flash setup, and field strategy for high-speed night bat photography using the **OM SYSTEM OM-1 Mark II** paired with the **M.Zuiko 12mm f/2.0** lens and off-camera **Backscatter HF-1** strobe.

---

## 1. Custom Mode 4 (C4) Camera Configuration

Optimized for instant shutter response and high depth of field using an off-camera manual strobe "Zone Trap".

### Primary Exposure & Drive
* **Shooting Mode:** **Manual (M)** – Hard locks ambient and strobe exposure control.
* **Shutter Speed:** **1/250s** – Maximum mechanical flash sync limit for the OM-1 Mark II.
* **Aperture:** **f/5.6 – f/8.0** – Maximizes depth of field (zone focus extends from ~1m to infinity at 12mm).
* **ISO:** **400 – 800** – Native clean gain that compensates for low flash power without noise penalty.
* **Drive Mode:** **Sequential Low (~10 fps)** or **Single Shot**
  * *Critical Notice:* **Pro Capture is BANNED**. Pro Cap physically disables flash triggering due to high burst rates.
* **Image Stabilization:** **S-IS AUTO** or **OFF** (when tripod-mounted).

### Focus Configuration (Zone Trap)
* **AF Mode:** **Manual Focus (MF)** – Set distance to **1.5m – 2.0m** and lock focus.
* **AF Illuminator:** **OFF**
* **Zone Focus Strategy:** At 12mm and f/5.6–f/8.0, the hyperfocal distance ensures everything within 1m to infinity remains sharply rendered, eliminating autofocus hunting in total darkness.

### Flash & Trigger Settings
* **Flash Mode:** **Fill-In (⚡)** – Ensures immediate full-sync triggering.
* **RC Mode:** **OFF** – **Crucial**. Turning RC Mode OFF eliminates optical command pre-flashes, preventing shutter lag so fast-flying bats stay in frame.
* **White Balance:** **5500K (Daylight)** or **Auto** – Color-matched to the flash pulse.

### Viewfinder & Display
* **Metering Mode:** **ESP (Digital ESP / Evaluative)** – Balanced background preview during dusk/twilight.
* **Night View / Live View Boost:** **On2 (Night View)** (`MENU` → `Custom Menu` → `2. Night View` → `On2`). Overrides exposure preview to amplify sensor gain in the EVF/LCD, allowing clear visibility of background foliage and flying silhouettes in near-total darkness.

## 3. Off-Camera Strobe & Lighting Mechanics

### Main Light (Backscatter HF-1)
* **Mounting:** Mounted off-camera on the **Manfrotto 055PROB Tripod** with **486RC2 Ball Head** at a **45° angle** to the targeted flight zone.
* **Modifier:** **Flat 140° Diffuser** or **Dome Diffuser** (softens wing texture and prevents harsh hotspotting).
* **Power Output:** **1/16 to 1/64 (Manual)**
  * *Ultra-Short Pulse:* At 1/16–1/64 power, the strobe burst duration shrinks to **~1/10,000s – 1/20,000s**, completely freezing wing motion.
  * *Rapid Recycling:* Low power draws minimal energy, allowing the HF-1 to fire continuously at **~10 fps** without skipping frames.
* **Pilot / Aiming Light:** **ON (Low/Medium Power)** – Acts as an optical "viewfinder target" in mid-air. When a bat enters the illuminated light cone, fire the shutter.

### Backlight / Translucency (Backscatter MF-2) — *Optional*
* **Positioning:** Placed behind the focus zone aimed slightly toward the camera.
* **Power Output:** **1/32 to 1/64 (Manual)**.
* **Effect:** Backlights translucent wing membranes and defines the bat's silhouette against the black night backdrop.

## 4. Environmental Timing & Conditions Checklist

1. **Target Time Window:** Civil Twilight (20–40 minutes after sunset, e.g., **9:20 PM – 10:00 PM** in August).
2. **Temperature:** Ideally **above 12°C – 14°C** (cold weather causes insects to drop, forcing bats into roost torpor).
3. **Wind & Sky:** Calm, still wind with high humidity or warm overcast dusk for peak insect hatches.
