This document provides a unified technical profile for configuring the **OM SYSTEM OM-1 Mark II** for two contrasting environments: Custom Mode 1 (C1) for dedicated macro photography and Custom Mode 2 (C2) for ultra-wide fisheye photography.

## 2. Shared Ergonomic Architecture (Muscle Memory Alignment)

Both profiles are mapped identically to the physical dials using the camera's Fn Lever. This ensures zero operational friction when swapping lenses and ports between dives; your fingers execute the same physical movements to alter exposure across both setups.

### Fn Lever: Position 1 (Primary Exposure Control)
* **Front Dial: Aperture Control**
  * *In C1 (Macro):* Adjusts between f/11 and f/14 to maximize depth of field while balancing diffraction.
  * *In C2 (Fisheye):* Opens up to f/1.8 for close-up portraits, or steps down to f/8.0 to guarantee sharp corners behind the dome glass.
* **Rear Dial: Shutter Speed Control**
  * *In C1 (Macro):* Locked at 1/250s to isolate the subject by converting ambient light into a black background.
  * *In C2 (Fisheye):* Brackets ambient light. Ranges from 1/60s (to absorb ambient emerald light) up to 1/250s (to clamp down on backscatter or muddy water).

### Fn Lever: Position 2 (Lighting Trim & Sensitivity)
* **Front Dial: Flash Exposure Compensation**
  * *In C1 & C2:* Quickly trims strobe output power up or down to manage highly reflective subjects or hotspots without adjusting physical strobe positions mid-dive.
* **Rear Dial: ISO Sensitivity**
  * *In C1 & C2:* Defaults to native ISO 200. Used as a quick modifier to boost exposure only when entering deep or intensely dark overhead canopies.

## 3. Core In-Water Deployment Strategies

### Managing the C1 Macro Environment
* **Trust the Limiter:** The 0.19m–0.40m physical lock prevents the lens from hunting into background water or open spaces.
* **Ambient Elimination:** The 1/250s mechanical shutter speed acts as a hard filter against ambient light, allowing the tight, localized SC Macro TTL flash profile to handle 100% of the exposure.

### Managing the C2 Fisheye Environment
* **The Dual-Exposure Principle:** Shutter speeds like 1/60s or 1/100s do not blur your foreground subject because the flash duration of the HF-1 is extremely fast (1/1000s to 1/5000s). The shutter remains open longer solely to act as an ambient light sponge for the background water column.
* **The Centimeter Rule:** An 8mm fisheye has an exceptionally short hyperfocal distance (~2.4 meters at f/1.8). To force a shallow depth of field and create background bokeh, you must position the dome port glass **within 10 to 20 centimeters** of your subject.
* **Dome Diffuser Geometry:** The dome diffuser pushes light wide (160°). Position the HF-1 **behind the housing handles** and angle it slightly outward ("toe-out"). This geometry ensures the edge of the light cone illuminates the subject while preventing direct flare or backscatter in front of the lens.
