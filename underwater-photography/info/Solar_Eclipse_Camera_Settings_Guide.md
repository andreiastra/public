## 🎯 Step-by-Step Shooting Workflow

1. **Mount & Secure Filter:**
   * Slide your custom **Explore Scientific Solarix DIY filter collar** firmly over the outer barrel of your M.Zuiko 100-400mm lens hood **before** pointing the camera at the Sun.
2. **Setup Location & Horizon:**
   * Position your Manfrotto tripod on firm ground with an unobstructed view toward the **West-Northwest (WNW)** horizon ($15.6^\circ$ altitude at peak).
3. **Acquire & Lock Focus:**
   * Set AF mode to **S-AF+MF**.
   * Half-press the shutter to let Single AF acquire the outer edge of the crescent Sun.
   * Turn the manual focus ring slightly to engage **MF Assist (Peaking / Magnification)** to verify micro-contrast sharpness along the crescent edge.
   * Leave focus locked once sharp.
4. **Dial Exposure & Test Shots:**
   * Set camera to **M Mode**: `f/8.0`, `ISO 200`, `1/1000s`.
   * Check exposure on the EVF/LCD histogram — ensure the solar disc is bright and sharp without overexposing the crescent edge.
5. **Capture Sequence:**
   * Take test exposures every 5–10 minutes leading up to maximum eclipse at **19:13 IST**.
