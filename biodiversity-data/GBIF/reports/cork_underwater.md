# Underwater species in West Cork on GBIF

> Generated by `scripts/cork_underwater.py` from live GBIF counts. Every recorder and dataset, not only `andreiastra`.

**446** marine species in **5080** photos in West Cork and its coastal waters. **76** of the species and **140** of the photos are mine. The area holds 24204 present occurrences with a photo, of every habitat. The photo counts can include copies of iNaturalist records that other datasets republish (counts cannot tell them apart), so they may be a little high.

## Filters applied

1. Inside the polygon around West Cork and its coastal waters: `geometry=POLYGON((-9.51086 51.84839,-10.38407 51.57121,-9.66406 51.32488,-8.37264 51.58549,-8.53962 51.86447,-9.51086 51.84839))`
2. Occurrence is present, not an absence record: `occurrenceStatus=PRESENT`
3. The record has a photo: `mediaType=StillImage`
4. Identified to species: counted per `speciesKey` with `facet=speciesKey`
5. Marine species, leaving out Aves and Magnoliopsida (seabirds and coastal plants): a GBIF species profile (WoRMS or Catalogue of Life) marks it as marine

The filters can be repeated on [gbif.org/occurrence/search](https://www.gbif.org/occurrence/search) (Location, the polygon above; Occurrence status, Present; Media type, Still image). The website can't filter by marine species, so filters 4 and 5 are applied by the script. My photos use the same filters plus `recordedBy=Andre Astra`.
