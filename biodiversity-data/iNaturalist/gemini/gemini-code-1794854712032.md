## 1. Executive Summary of Observations

Your dataset spans **479 total observations across 296 species**, combining a core portfolio of **129 subtidal SCUBA records across 20 West Cork dive sites** with seasonal **coastal wetland and inland terrestrial sightings**[cite: 2, 5].

* **Ecological Breadth:** Captures a continuous gradient from sheltered sea-lough soft sediments (Lough Hyne) and exposed Atlantic bedrock reefs (Gortdubh Pier) to estuarine wader feeding grounds (Courtmacsherry, Rosscarbery) and inland Cork habitats (Coolanagh)[cite: 2].
* **Open Science Pipeline:** All records use open licensing (`CC BY-NC`) and open geoprivacy, enabling automatic weekly synchronization of **Research Grade** sightings to the **Global Biodiversity Information Facility (GBIF)** and Ireland's **National Biodiversity Data Centre (NBDC)**[cite: 5, 10].
* **Verification Velocity:** Terrestrial avian and lepidopteran observations routinely achieve **Research Grade** within 30–60 minutes of upload, whereas cryptic marine invertebrates (opisthobranchs, spider crabs, and sponges) require targeted diagnostic crops and specialist tagging (`@username`)[cite: 2, 11].

## 3. High-Value Scientific & Conservation Sightings

### 1. Documented Trophic Interaction (Highest Ecological Value)
* **Football Jersey Worm preying on Fan Worm ([Obs #395271306](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/395271306)):** Direct photographic documentation of *Tubulanus annulatus* extracting a sabellid fan worm from its tube[cite: 2, 12]. Inter-polychaete predation is rarely recorded in citizen science and feeds directly into global food-web repositories such as **Global Biotic Interactions (GloBI)**[cite: 2].

### 2. Globally & Regionally Rare Marine Taxa
* **Lobe Shell (*Philine quadripartita*):** Cryptic sand-dwelling bubble snail logged in Lough Hyne ([Obs #396151006](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/396151006), [#395268141](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/395268141)), representing one of only ~143 global observations on iNaturalist[cite: 2, 9].
* **Pimplet Anemone (*Anthopleura ballii*):** Logged at 5 m in Lough Hyne ([Obs #396151001](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/396151001); ~513 global records)[cite: 2, 9].
* **Connemarra Clingfish (*Lepadogaster candolii*):** Crytobenthic crevice-dwelling fish recorded at Zetland Pier ([Obs #395276891](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/395276891))[cite: 2].
* **Specialist Opisthobranchs:** *Facelina annulicornis* (Kilcrohane Pier, Obs [#395248465](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/395248465), [#395252016](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/395252016)), *Polycera faeroensis* (Seven Heads Pier, Obs [#395247782](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/395247782)), and *Aeolidiella alderi* / *A. glauca* (Lough Hyne, Obs [#395257807](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/395257807), [#395257532](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/395257532))[cite: 2].

### 3. Coastal Conservation & Terrestrial Phenology
* **IUCN Near Threatened (NT) Waders:** Un-obscured foraging locations at Courtmacsherry and Rosscarbery estuaries for **Black-tailed Godwit** (*Limosa limosa*, [Obs #396783242](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/396783242)) and **Eurasian Oystercatcher** (*Haematopus ostralegus*, [Obs #396783240](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/396783240), [#396782660](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/396782660)), directly supporting Irish wetland waterbird monitoring (I-WeBS)[cite: 2].
* **Seasonal Migration & Phenology:** Late-summer migrant **Eurasian Hummingbird Hawkmoth** (*Macroglossum stellatarum*, [Obs #396871636](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/396871636)) in Coolanagh, wintering waterfowl assemblages (**Eurasian Wigeon**, *Mareca penelope*, [Obs #396783244](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/396783244)), and resident passerines (**European Starling**, **Great Tit**, **House Sparrow**)[cite: 2].
